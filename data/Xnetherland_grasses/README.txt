Heinen et al., 2020 -- netherland_grasses 

Short description:
Looked at soil legacy based on which plants were there before. 192 plots all got some unique treatment of grasses or forbs for **DIFFERENT DURATIONS**. Then looked at soil microbiome. 

Methods 
- Treated soil with plants; dif concentrations for dif amounts of time -- I think the amount of time could be an environmental variable, maybe? 
Technically 96 plots, each divided into 2 sub-plots; 24 unique communities (if you were to consider how much of each species was added). 
- Species: Achillea millefolium, Jocobaea vulgaris, Plantago lanceolata, Taraxacu officinale, Agrostis capillaris, Holcus lanaus, Lolium perenne, Phleum pratens (8 species). 
- 1 and 2 year lags  

Go to De Long et al. 2019 to see which block got which seed mixture. Produced a treatment spreadsheet based on information from a figure. We'll treat the 'forb_percentage' as an environment type, at least the 75 and 25%.
