Strahan et al., 2018 -- greenhouse_cats 

Short description: 
Manipulated light and soil properties to determine coexistence outcomes of 5 grass species. All pots got the same species mixes (equal densities), just grown in dif environments 

Methods 
- Species: Bouteloua gracilis, Muhlenbergia montana, Elymus elymoides, Festuca arizonica, Poa fendleriana 
- Environmental levels: soil (limestone, basalt), light (low, high) 
- 66 pots in the analysis
